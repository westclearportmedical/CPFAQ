The CP-100 V4

One clear cover for the whole external line

The hub, extension legs, clamps and connectors sit in a single low-profile housing on the upper arm, worn over the standard dressing and securement.

Concept render. Patent pending.

Key facts

Worn over today's dressing and securementAdds protection on top of existing securement and the transparent dressing. Replaces neither.
No contact with the fluid pathNothing connects to the catheter or touches the insertion site.
Changed with the weekly dressingApplied at a visit the nurse already makes. No extra trip.
